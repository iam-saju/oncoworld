54M, ECOG 1, with a rapidly enlarging right cervical mass, dysphagia and morning facial fullness; 12.0 cm anterior mediastinal mass with about 50% SVC narrowing, all disease above the diaphragm, LDH 740 rising to 812, uric acid 7.6 to 7.9, LVEF 60%. Node biopsy was signed out as DLBCL NOS (GCB, MYC 80%/BCL2 90% by IHC, Ki-67 95%), but the 9/19 FISH addendum shows MYC and BCL2 rearrangements after R-CHOP x6 had already been planned to start 9/22.
What this case tests: The late FISH addendum changes the diagnosis to double-hit lymphoma (HGBL with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements), not DLBCL NOS. | The R-CHOP plan was written before FISH and must be replaced with intensive therapy such as DA-EPOCH-R x6 or a clinical trial. | Disease is stage II bulky (12 cm mediastinal mass, above the diaphragm only), not stage III or IV. | IPI and CNS-IPI are only 1, but double-hit biology drives the risk, so brain MRI, lumbar puncture and CNS prophylaxis are still needed. | Bulky mass, high LDH and rising uric acid need TLS prophylaxis; DA-EPOCH-R needs G-CSF every cycle and its 96-hour infusion goes through the new port.
