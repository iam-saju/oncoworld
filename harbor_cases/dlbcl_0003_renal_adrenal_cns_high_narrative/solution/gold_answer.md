# Gold Answer

## Diagnosis / Cell Of Origin

- Biopsy establishes diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, NOS.
- CD10+ phenotype supports germinal-center B-cell phenotype by Hans algorithm.

## Stage

- Kidney, adrenal, and liver lesions are non-lymphatic extranodal lymphoma sites.
- This is Lugano/Ann Arbor stage IV disease.

## MYC/BCL2 Biology

- MYC expression is 20% and BCL2 expression is 30%, below common double-expressor thresholds.
- MYC/BCL2/BCL6 rearrangements are absent by FISH.
- Therefore this is neither double-expressor lymphoma nor double-hit lymphoma.

## Prognosis

- Calculate IPI using age, stage, LDH, ECOG, and extranodal sites.
- This patient has multiple adverse IPI features: age >60, stage IV, elevated LDH, ECOG 2, and multiple extranodal sites.
- Calculate CNS-IPI separately.

## First-Line Treatment

- The patient has advanced DLBCL and appears to need curative-intent immunochemotherapy if organ function permits.
- Pola-R-CHP or R-CHOP are appropriate first-line options.
- DA-EPOCH-R is not required by double-hit biology because FISH is negative.

## CNS Risk

- Kidney and adrenal involvement are high-risk CNS-IPI anatomical features.
- With age >60, stage IV, elevated LDH, ECOG 2, multiple extranodal sites, and kidney/adrenal involvement, CNS-IPI is high.
- CNS staging and prophylaxis should be strongly considered, with renal function affecting high-dose methotrexate feasibility.

## Response Assessment

- DLBCL is FDG-avid.
- Use FDG PET-CT with Lugano/Deauville criteria, with end-of-treatment PET as the key response assessment.
