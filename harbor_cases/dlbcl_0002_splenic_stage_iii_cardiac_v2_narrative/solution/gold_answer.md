## diagnosis

Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, NOS, non-germinal-center (non-GCB) phenotype by the Hans algorithm (CD10-, BCL6+, MUM1+). Hans is an immunohistochemical surrogate and is not equivalent to molecular ABC cell-of-origin by gene-expression profiling. EBER negative; CD5 negative. The left axillary node is lymphoma, not recurrent breast carcinoma: cytokeratin AE1/AE3 and GATA3 are negative and there is no epithelial component.

## stage

Lugano (Ann Arbor) stage III with B symptoms (documented fevers to 38.4 C and drenching night sweats; weight loss ~3%, below 10%): FDG-avid nodes on both sides of the diaphragm (left axillary and mediastinal above; mesenteric and para-aortic below) with splenic involvement. The spleen is lymphatic tissue (a nodal site, not extranodal), so splenic nodules do not make this stage IV; there is no kidney, adrenal, liver, lung, bone, breast or CNS involvement and the bone marrow biopsy is negative. Largest mass 7.5 cm (mesenteric): below the 10 cm Lugano threshold for bulk, although some trials define bulk at 7.5 cm, so the definition varies.

## biology

Double-expressor lymphoma by IHC (MYC 45%, at or above the 40% threshold; BCL2 70%, above the 50% threshold), which carries a somewhat worse prognosis with standard immunochemotherapy. Not double-hit: FISH shows no MYC, BCL2 or BCL6 rearrangement, so this is not high-grade B-cell lymphoma. Double-expressor status alone does not justify DA-EPOCH-R or other intensified regimens. Ki-67 ~80%.

## prognosis

IPI 2 of 5 (low-intermediate risk): LDH elevated (510 U/L, ULN 225) and stage III. Age 59 (not >60), ECOG 1 and 0 extranodal sites (the spleen is not counted as an extranodal site) score 0. CNS-IPI 2 of 6 (intermediate risk): LDH and stage only; no kidney/adrenal involvement. Beta-2 microglobulin mildly elevated at 2.9 mg/L.

## first_line_treatment

Curative intent. The key issue is cardiac: she received cumulative doxorubicin 240 mg/m2 (dose-dense AC-T, 2016) plus left breast radiotherapy, and LVEF has fallen from 62% (2016) to 45% with GLS -14% and mild global hypokinesis, a >10-point decline to <50% consistent with cancer therapy-related cardiac dysfunction (asymptomatic, NYHA I). Standard full-dose doxorubicin (R-CHOP or Pola-R-CHP) should not be given in this setting. Urgent cardio-oncology referral before cycle 1, with guideline-directed cardioprotection: start an ACE inhibitor or ARB (e.g. switch amlodipine to lisinopril or candesartan) plus a beta-blocker (carvedilol), titrated as blood pressure allows. Recommend curative-intent R-CEOP (rituximab, cyclophosphamide, etoposide, vincristine, prednisone; etoposide substituted for doxorubicin) for 6 cycles as an anthracycline-free regimen; R-GCVP (gemcitabine in place of doxorubicin) is an alternative. Tell the patient that anthracycline-free regimens likely have lower efficacy (cure rates) than anthracycline-based therapy, based on retrospective data. An anthracycline-modified option (R-CHOP with liposomal doxorubicin or with dexrazoxane) is acceptable only if cardio-oncology judges the cardiac risk acceptable after evaluation and optimization. Monitor during therapy with serial echocardiography (LVEF and GLS) every 2-3 cycles and at end of treatment, plus high-sensitivity troponin and NT-proBNP at baseline and with each cycle. DA-EPOCH-R is not indicated: double-expressor status alone does not justify intensification, and it also contains doxorubicin.

## cns_risk

Intermediate CNS-relapse risk (CNS-IPI 2). No high-risk anatomic sites: no kidney, adrenal, testicular or breast involvement (the axillary node is lymphoma and the breasts show no uptake), and she has no neurological symptoms. No routine CNS prophylaxis is recommended (no high-dose methotrexate, no intrathecal therapy). MRI brain and lumbar puncture only if neurological symptoms develop.

## response_assessment

End-of-treatment FDG PET-CT 6-8 weeks after the last cycle, read with Lugano 2014 criteria and the Deauville 5-point scale (Deauville 1-3 = complete metabolic response). Interim PET-CT after 2-4 cycles is optional and should not change therapy unless it shows progression. Biopsy residual Deauville 4-5 uptake if it would change management. Repeat echocardiogram at end of treatment and during survivorship.

## supportive_care

Tumor lysis risk is low-intermediate (LDH ~2.3x ULN, largest mass 7.5 cm, normal creatinine and uric acid): oral hydration and allopurinol starting before cycle 1, with TLS labs at 24-72 hours. Primary G-CSF (pegfilgrastim) with each cycle is reasonable given the febrile neutropenia risk of R-CEOP/R-GCVP and to avoid dose delays. Anastrozole has no meaningful interaction with lymphoma therapy and can continue to complete year 10, or be held during chemotherapy if breast oncology prefers; coordinate with her breast oncologist. Blood pressure (148/90) is controlled with the ACE inhibitor/ARB plus beta-blocker in place of amlodipine. Antiemetics per regimen. Bone health: osteopenia on an aromatase inhibitor plus steroid pulses, continue calcium/vitamin D.

Fit for curative therapy: ECOG 1 (disease-related fatigue and fevers), age 59, normal renal and hepatic function, no heart-failure symptoms (NYHA I). The reduced LVEF changes the regimen, not the curative goal. Her goal is cure and a return to full-time work; explain the trade-off between cardiac safety and the possibly lower efficacy of an anthracycline-free regimen, and decide together with cardio-oncology input. Her worry about her heart (mother with heart failure) is addressed by the cardio-oncology plan and cardioprotection.
