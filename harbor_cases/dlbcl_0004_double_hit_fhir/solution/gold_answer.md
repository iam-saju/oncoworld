# Gold Answer

## Diagnosis / Cell Of Origin

- Biopsy establishes aggressive large B-cell lymphoma.
- CD10+ phenotype supports GCB phenotype by Hans algorithm.
- With MYC and BCL2 rearrangements, the appropriate biological classification is high-grade B-cell lymphoma with MYC and BCL2 rearrangements, often called double-hit lymphoma.

## Stage

- Disease is confined to nodal regions above the diaphragm: cervical and mediastinal.
- There is no disease below the diaphragm and marrow is negative.
- This is Lugano/Ann Arbor stage II bulky disease.

## MYC/BCL2 Biology

- MYC and BCL2 protein expression are high by IHC, so the tumor is also double-expressor by protein criteria.
- More importantly, MYC and BCL2 rearrangements are detected by FISH.
- This is rearrangement-defined double-hit lymphoma, not merely double-expressor lymphoma.

## Prognosis

- Calculate IPI using age, stage, LDH, ECOG, and extranodal sites.
- Elevated LDH and bulky disease are adverse features.
- Double-hit genetics are a major adverse biological risk factor.
- Calculate CNS-IPI and consider double-hit biology in CNS-risk discussion, even though kidney/adrenal/testicular/breast sites are absent.

## First-Line Treatment

- The patient needs curative-intent intensive immunochemotherapy if fit.
- DA-EPOCH-R or clinical-trial enrollment is appropriate for double-hit lymphoma.
- R-CHOP alone is generally not the preferred regimen for rearrangement-defined double-hit lymphoma.

## CNS Risk

- CNS-IPI should be calculated.
- Kidney/adrenal, testicular, and breast involvement are absent, but double-hit biology and elevated LDH support careful CNS-risk assessment.
- CNS prophylaxis should be considered, not mandated without individualized risk and renal/fitness review.

## Response Assessment

- DLBCL/HGBL is FDG-avid.
- Use FDG PET-CT with Lugano/Deauville criteria and end-of-treatment PET.
