# Gold Answer

## Diagnosis / Cell Of Origin

- Lymph-node biopsy establishes diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, NOS.
- CD10-, BCL6+, MUM1+ is non-GCB phenotype by Hans algorithm.
- Hans non-GCB phenotype is not synonymous with definitive molecular ABC subtype.

## Stage

- Nodal disease is present above and below the diaphragm.
- Splenic involvement is present.
- Bone marrow is negative.
- The best stage from the supplied disease map is Lugano/Ann Arbor stage III with bulky disease and B symptoms.

## MYC/BCL2 Biology

- MYC expression is 50% and BCL2 expression is 80%.
- These values meet commonly used double-expressor lymphoma thresholds.
- MYC/BCL2/BCL6 rearrangements are absent by FISH.
- Therefore this is double-expressor lymphoma, not rearrangement-defined double-hit lymphoma.

## Prognosis

- Calculate IPI using age, stage, LDH, ECOG, and extranodal sites.
- Supplied IPI factors: age >60, elevated LDH, advanced stage, ECOG 1.
- With no confirmed non-lymphatic extranodal disease in the supplied facts, IPI is 3.
- Calculate CNS-IPI separately.
- CNS-IPI requires CNS-risk anatomical review, including kidney/adrenal involvement and high-risk extranodal sites.

## First-Line Treatment

- The patient appears fit for curative-intent immunochemotherapy.
- Pola-R-CHP for 6 cycles is an appropriate contemporary first-line option for higher-risk DLBCL.
- R-CHOP for 6 cycles remains an acceptable alternative.
- Double-expressor lymphoma alone does not mandate DA-EPOCH-R.

## CNS Risk

- Calculate CNS-IPI rather than relying on double-expressor status alone.
- Determine kidney/adrenal involvement, total extranodal-site count, testicular or breast involvement, and other high-risk anatomical sites.
- CNS prophylaxis is not automatically indicated from the supplied data.

## Response Assessment

- DLBCL is FDG-avid.
- Use FDG PET-CT with Lugano/Deauville 5-point criteria.
- End-of-treatment PET is the key response assessment.
- If residual PET-positive disease would trigger salvage therapy, biopsy should be considered when feasible.
