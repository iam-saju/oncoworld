SURGICAL PATHOLOGY: CT-GUIDED CORE BIOPSY, RETROPERITONEAL MASS, 2027-04-28

Specimen: core biopsies of the retroperitoneal mass, adequate for diagnosis.
Immunohistochemistry: CD20 positive. CD19 positive. MYC 20% of tumor cells. BCL2 30% of tumor cells.

FISH: MYC: no rearrangement detected. BCL2: no rearrangement detected.
DIAGNOSIS: Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, germinal center B-cell (GCB) phenotype, in a patient with
