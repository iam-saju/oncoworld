# Gold Answer

## Diagnosis / Cell Of Origin

- Excisional lymph-node biopsy establishes diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, NOS.
- CD10+ and BCL6+ with MUM1- supports GCB phenotype by Hans algorithm.

## Stage

- Disease is limited to a single cervical lymph-node region.
- There are no extranodal sites, no bulky mass, and bone marrow is negative.
- This is Lugano/Ann Arbor stage I non-bulky disease.

## MYC/BCL2 Biology

- MYC 15% and BCL2 30% do not meet common double-expressor thresholds.
- MYC/BCL2/BCL6 rearrangements are absent by FISH.
- Therefore this is neither double-expressor nor double-hit lymphoma.

## Prognosis

- Calculate IPI and CNS-IPI.
- Age <60, normal LDH, stage I, ECOG 0, and no extranodal disease indicate low-risk IPI.
- CNS-IPI is low and there are no high-risk CNS anatomical sites.

## First-Line Treatment

- Limited-stage non-bulky DLBCL should be treated with curative-intent immunochemotherapy.
- Appropriate options include abbreviated R-CHOP followed by involved-site radiotherapy, PET-adapted therapy, or full-course R-CHOP depending on local standard and patient factors.
- DA-EPOCH-R, CNS prophylaxis, or double-hit-directed intensification are not indicated from the supplied facts.

## CNS Risk

- CNS relapse risk is low by CNS-IPI.
- Kidney/adrenal, testicular, breast, marrow, and other high-risk sites are absent.
- CNS prophylaxis is not indicated from the supplied data.

## Response Assessment

- DLBCL is FDG-avid.
- Use FDG PET-CT with Lugano/Deauville criteria.
- End-of-treatment PET is the key response assessment.
