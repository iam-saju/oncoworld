# OncoWorld Vendor Prep Plan

This plan turns the transcript into a practical readiness checklist for an oncology evaluation pilot.

## What the call means

The route is supplier onboarding first, not an immediate purchase commitment. OncoWorld should prepare a legitimate business entity, private benchmark material, physician-reviewed cases, and a small submission package before asking for a business-development introduction.

## Core gates

1. Legal entity
   - Incorporate before formal onboarding.
   - Prepare legal company name, place of incorporation, and notice email.
   - Keep basic due-diligence details ready.

2. Confidentiality and rights
   - Do not share private case content before an NDA or equivalent written terms.
   - Clarify who owns paid cases, whether samples can be reused, and whether delivered tasks can be used for model training or only evaluation.
   - Put physician reviewers under confidentiality and rights-assignment terms.

3. Clinical quality
   - Build synthetic cases with oncology specialist review.
   - Review both the case content and the case-specific rubric.
   - Store sign-off records and reviewer notes.

4. Submission package
   - Prepare around five excellent examples first.
   - Use the required benchmark format once confirmed.
   - Include rubric rationale, expected answer behavior, and failure-mode notes.

5. Baseline testing
   - Run public model baselines before reconnecting.
   - Record model ID, date, settings, pass/fail outcome, and reviewer comments.
   - Avoid sending confidential benchmark material through free tiers unless the provider terms protect the content.

## Recommended pilot shape

- Five sample tasks for initial quality review.
- A target production batch of about 100 cases if the sample is accepted.
- Two or more physician review passes for each case and rubric.
- A fixed evidence log covering provenance, review status, baseline results, and revision history.

## Message discipline

Public site copy should describe OncoWorld's capability without presenting any lab's process as a guaranteed deal. Use language like "vendor-ready", "NDA-first", "pilot package", and "frontier model labs" rather than naming private procurement details unless there is permission to do so.
